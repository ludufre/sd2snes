sd2snes+ menu music  --  /sd2snes/music/
==========================================

Every .spc in this folder is a candidate for the menu's random music mode
(config.yml: MenuMusicRandom / MenuMusicFolder).  Drop your own .spc files here
to add them to the draw, or delete the ones you do not like.  /sd2snes/menu.spc
(a copy of "28") is the fallback when this folder is empty or random is off.
